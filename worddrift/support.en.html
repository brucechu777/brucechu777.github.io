Word Drift Support

How to play

  1. Gather 3 to 12 people and one phone.
  2. Pass the phone around and secretly look at your own word. Most players share the same word; the odd ones get a slightly different one.
  3. Talk it over, giving hints without saying your word outright.
  4. When time is up, everyone votes for who they think the odd one is.
  5. In Blank Card mode, one player draws a blank card with no word. If that is you, blend in.

With 3 to 6 players there is 1 odd one; with 7 to 12 there are 2. Discussion time can be set from 1 to 10 minutes in Settings.

Restore purchases

If you bought Remove Ads and switched devices or reinstalled, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID (App Store) or Google account (Google Play) you bought with.
