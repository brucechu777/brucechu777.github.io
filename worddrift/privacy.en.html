Word Drift Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Summary

Word Drift is a pass-the-phone word game for a group. It has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, and the developer runs no server.

What stays on your device

  • Player names and languages
  • Which word pairs you have already played
  • Game settings (mode, discussion time, sound and haptics)
  • Whether you have purchased Remove Ads

This stays on your device and is never sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes it.

Ads

The free version shows ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). This is the only third-party service in the app. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use data such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information and ad interactions, as described by Google:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Before ads first appear you may see a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). On iOS you may also see the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can decline either one, and change your choice later. Where a privacy options entry is required, it appears in the app's Settings.

Remove Ads

If you buy Remove Ads (one-time, $2.99 / ¥480), the ads SDK is not initialized and the app makes no ad-related requests to Google. The purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play; the developer never receives your payment details.
