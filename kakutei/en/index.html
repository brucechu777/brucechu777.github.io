Filing your Japanese tax return in the NTA Sakusei Corner, step by step (sole proprietors)

This page follows the National Tax Agency's 確定申告書等作成コーナー (the Sakusei Corner) as it appears for tax year Reiwa 7, and walks a sole proprietor with business income through filing. Japanese screen names are kept verbatim so you can match them on screen.

Disclaimer: this page only summarizes general rules published by the National Tax Agency (NTA) and other official bodies. It is not tax advice for any individual's situation and does not file anything for you. For individual cases, ask your tax office (税務署) or a tax accountant (税理士).

Who has to file

  • If you have one employer: you must file when your income other than salary and retirement pay totals more than ¥200,000.
  • If you have two or more employers: you must file when the wages not covered by year-end adjustment plus other income exceed ¥200,000.
  • Exception: if your salary after the salary deduction is ¥1.5 million or less and your side income is ¥200,000 or less, filing may not be required.
  • The ¥200,000 is 所得 (income after expenses), not 収入 (gross receipts).
  • Sole proprietors (business income) generally need to file. We did not directly confirm this on an NTA page, so rely on your tax office's guidance.
  • Resident tax (住民税) follows different rules from income tax; see the side-income page.

Deadline

  • Tax year Reiwa 7 (filed in 2026): income tax filing and payment due 2026-03-16 (3/15 was a Sunday); consumption tax due 3/31.
  • Tax year Reiwa 8 (filed in 2027): expected 2027-03-15 (a Monday). This is an estimate based on the usual schedule; follow the NTA's announcement. The NTA has not published that year's page yet.
  • The blue-return special deduction (¥550,000/¥650,000) requires filing by the deadline.

White return vs blue return (¥100,000)

  • White return: you must keep books (date, counterparty, amount; daily totals allowed) and submit a 収支内訳書.
  • Blue return: you submit a 青色申告決算書. The ¥100,000 deduction goes with simple bookkeeping; ¥550,000/¥650,000 require double-entry bookkeeping (journal, ledger, balance sheet/P&L) plus further conditions. This page covers only ¥100,000.
  • Blue returns need an advance filing of 青色申告承認申請書: within 2 months of starting a business, otherwise usually by 3/15 of the year before (from Yayoi/freee explanations, i.e. secondary; we did not read the NTA original).
  • Record retention: statutory books 7 years; other documents (financial statements, invoices, receipts) 5 years; electronic storage is allowed.

What to prepare

  • Your records of sales, purchases and each expense for the year; opening and closing inventory (棚卸高); and breakdowns for depreciation, wages, rent and interest if you have them.
  • If you have salary income: the 源泉徴収票 (withholding slip), used for the salary income screen.
  • Papers for deductions: social insurance premiums, 小規模企業共済 contributions, life and earthquake insurance, medical expenses, donations (including ふるさと納税), matching the items on the 控除の入力 screens.
  • For e-Tax filing (マイナンバーカード方式): your My Number Card, the マイナポータル app on your phone, and the 4-digit PIN for the 利用者証明用電子証明書 (three wrong tries in a row lock it).
  • Last year's saved .data file, if any: choose 「保存データを利用して作成」 on the start page to skip re-entering your personal details.

From 作成開始 to completion, step by step

  1. On the Sakusei Corner start page, press 「作成開始」.
  2. 「事前確認」: answer a few questions and choose how to submit: e-Tax (マイナンバーカード方式 or ID・パスワード方式) or 「印刷して書面提出」. Then choose whether to use マイナポータル連携.
  3. 「作成する申告書等の選択」: with business income, choose 「決算書・収支内訳書(+所得税)」. If you already made the statements in accounting software and only need to enter the business income amount, choose 「所得税」.
  4. On 「決算書・収支内訳書の作成」 press 「次へ進む」, then on 「作成する決算書・収支内訳書の選択」 pick one of 「青色申告決算書」, 「収支内訳書」 or 「青色申告決算書(現金主義用)」.
  5. On the type-selection screen choose 「営業等所得がある方」 (other choices cover farming, real estate and 雑所得(業務)).

White return: 「収支内訳書(一般用・営業等)の入力」

  • 収入金額: 売上(収入)金額 (press 「入力」 for the sub-screen; per customer, up to 4, the rest go into 「上記以外の売上先の計」), 家事消費, その他の収入.
  • 売上原価: 期首商品(製品)棚卸高, 仕入金額 (sub-screen, per supplier), 期末商品(製品)棚卸高.
  • 経費: 11 給料賃金, 12 外注工賃, 13 減価償却費, 14 貸倒金, 15 地代家賃, 16 利子割引料 (給料賃金, 減価償却費, 地代家賃 and 利子割引料 use the 「入力」 sub-screen).
  • 「その他の経費」 イ to レ are typed directly: 租税公課, 荷造運賃, 水道光熱費, 旅費交通費, 通信費, 広告宣伝費, 接待交際費, 損害保険料, 修繕費, 消耗品費, 福利厚生費, optional items, 雑費.
  • The screen shows 17 その他の経費の小計, 18 経費合計 and 19 専従者控除前の所得金額. Buttons: 「ここまでの入力内容を保存する」 (saves a .data file), 「前に戻る」, 「次へ進む」. It ends at 「所得金額の確認」.

操作の手引き 収支内訳書作成(白色申告)編

Blue ¥100,000: 「決算書(一般用)の入力」

  • The period defaults to 1/1 to 12/31. 1 売上(収入)金額 opens a monthly screen via 「入力」 (monthly or a yearly total, not both).
  • 売上原価: 2 期首商品(製品)棚卸高, 3 仕入金額, 4 小計, 5 期末商品(製品)棚卸高, 6 差引原価, 7 差引金額.
  • 経費 8 to 31: 8 租税公課, 9 荷造運賃, 10 水道光熱費, 11 旅費交通費, 12 通信費, 13 広告宣伝費, 14 接待交際費, 15 損害保険料, 16 修繕費, 17 消耗品費, 18 減価償却費, 19 福利厚生費, 20 給料賃金, 21 外注工賃, 22 利子割引料, 23 地代家賃, 24 貸倒金, 25 to 30 optional, 31 雑費; then 32 経費合計 and 33 差引金額.
  • It ends with 43 青色申告特別控除前の所得金額.
  • 「青色申告特別控除の入力」: pick 10万円, 55万円 or 65万円, and answer 「貸借対照表の作成を行うか」. With ¥100,000 you can choose not to make a balance sheet (the P&L is still required) and go straight to 「所得金額の確認」.

Then the income tax return and sending

  1. After the statements, the top step bar runs 「申告準備 > 決算書等の作成 > 住所等入力 > データ保存等」, then 「電子申告等データ内容確認後の作業について」 → 「引継ぎ情報等の確認」 (the business income carries into the income tax return).
  2. 「申告する所得の選択等」: tick the kinds of income (事業所得, 給与 and so on).
  3. 「収入・所得の入力」: press 「>」 next to each income; enter salary from the 源泉徴収票.
  4. 「控除の入力(1/2)」: 社会保険料控除, 小規模企業共済等掛金控除, 生命保険料控除, 地震保険料控除, 雑損控除・災害減免, 医療費控除, 寄附金控除 (including ふるさと納税 one-stop special cases, which must be entered when you file) and more. 「控除の入力(2/2)」: 配偶者(特別)控除, 扶養控除, 寡婦・ひとり親控除, 勤労学生控除, 障害者, 住宅借入金等特別控除 and more.
  5. 「計算結果の確認」, then 「還付方法等の入力」 for a refund or 「納付方法等の入力」 for tax due.
  6. 「財産債務調書、住民税等に関する事項」: once you file, no separate resident/business tax return is needed, but items treated differently from income tax must be ticked and filled in.
  7. 「基本情報の入力」 (address, name) then 「マイナンバーの入力」.
  8. Sending: 「送信前の申告内容確認」 (nothing is sent yet), 「送信準備」, 「申告書の送信」, 「送信結果の確認」 (shows 「正常に送信が完了しました。」), 「送信票等印刷へ進む」, 「申告書等送信票(兼送付書)等の確認」, 「申告書等を表示する」 for your copy. You can also download a .data file for next year.
  9. Afterwards: only attachments marked ○ in the 別途提出 column of the 送信票(兼送付書) need to be mailed, or you can send them later as PDF image data.
