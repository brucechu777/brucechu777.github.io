Escape Game: Sweets Shops Support

How to play

  • Use the left and right arrows to look around, and tap anything that catches your eye.
  • Tap a customer to hear their order. Put the toppings or treats on the plate, glass or box in exactly that order; a wrong order sends them back to your item bar.
  • Items you find go into the item bar at the bottom. Select one, then tap where you want to use it.

Hints

Tap the lightbulb (top right) for a hint. The last hint of each room is shown after a video ad, or straight away once you have bought Remove Ads.

Restore purchases

If you bought Remove Ads and see ads again after changing or reinstalling, open the menu (top left) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID / Google account you bought with.
