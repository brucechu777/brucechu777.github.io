Privacy Policy – Last Table: Server Tip Tracker

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Summary

A tip log for restaurant servers and bartenders. We receive no personal data: no account, no server of ours, no ads, no analytics or crash reporting added by us. On Android, however, the Google library used for the purchase may send technical information to Google (see “Purchases”). This app keeps records only. It is not tax advice and is not affiliated with the IRS.

What stays on your device

Your shifts, tips, tip-outs, workplaces and settings are saved only on your phone. We never receive them. Android’s own system backup (Google) or iCloud Backup (iPhone) may keep a copy of the app’s data under your own Google or Apple account and its policies; we cannot see it.

Files you save yourself

When you export a PDF tip diary or a CSV spreadsheet, or save a backup file, the file goes where you choose (for example Files, Downloads, email or a cloud drive you pick). We do not receive these files. What happens to them afterwards is up to you.

No ads, no account

The app shows no ads and has no sign-in or account.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is processed by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iPhone). We receive no payment details; the app only asks the store whether you own the unlock. Google Play or Apple keeps the purchase history under its own policy.

On Android, purchases use the Google Play Billing Library. Even if you never open the purchase screen (for example at app start), it may send technical information to Google: app package name and version, library version, device maker and model, OS version and build, country and language settings, time zone, carrier code, network type and purchase-processing status (success or failure). Your shifts and tips are not included. We never receive this information; Google handles it under its Privacy Policy.

Ratings

The app may show the system rating dialog (Google Play or App Store). Ratings are handled by Google or Apple.
