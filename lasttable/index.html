What the app does

Last Table is a tip log for restaurant servers and bartenders in the US. Log a shift in one screen: cash tips, card tips, non-cash tips, hours, and who you tipped out (by dollar amount or % of sales). It shows your weekly, monthly and yearly take-home tips. There is no account, no ads and no subscription, and your shifts stay on your phone.

Free and paid

Free, always: log, edit and delete unlimited shifts; full history; weekly, monthly and yearly totals; tip-outs by $ or %; spreadsheet (CSV) export; backup file and restore.

One-time unlock (US$9.99): a day-by-day tip diary PDF, and more than one workplace (your first workplace is free). One purchase, yours to keep. It is not a subscription.

Back up your shifts

There are two ways, and you can use both:

  • Phone backup. On iPhone, your shifts are included in iCloud Backup (Settings › your name › iCloud › iCloud Backup). On Android, they are included in your phone's own backup to your Google account (Settings › Google › Backup). Set up a new phone from that backup and they come back. These backups run on the phone's own schedule, so a shift you logged a few minutes ago may not be in them yet.
  • Backup file. In the app, open Settings › Backup & restore › Save backup file. It saves a normal spreadsheet (CSV) wherever you choose: Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email. Save a new one from time to time; the home screen shows how long ago you saved the last one.

Restore your shifts

Install Last Table on the new phone, open Settings › Backup & restore › Restore from a file, and pick a backup file you saved. The app shows how many shifts will be added before anything changes. Shifts already on the phone are kept and never doubled, so restoring the same file twice adds nothing.

Restore your purchase

On a new phone or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple Account (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you used to buy, open Settings and tap Restore purchase. You are never charged twice for the same account.
