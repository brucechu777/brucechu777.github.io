My Number card / Japanese passport photo size 35×45 mm — official requirements and konbini printing

Official size: 35 × 45 mm (width × height). Head size: for the Japanese passport (MOFA) the top of the head to the chin is 34 ± 2 mm. The My Number card page gives no figure in mm, so the app uses the stricter passport value. Background: none; no objects, shadows or patterns. White is recommended for the passport (MOFA). The app makes it white. Validity: taken within 6 months before the application (passport) or within the last 6 months (My Number card). Source: 外務省 (MOFA) / J-LIS (see the source links below; checked 2026-11-18).

Other conditions

  • No hat
  • Face straight to the camera
  • Glasses frames over the eyes, or light glare on the lenses, are not acceptable
  • Photos edited in a way that gets in the way of in-person identification are not acceptable
  • Passport: write your name on the back, pressing lightly so it does not show through on the front
  • My Number card: write your name and date of birth on the back
  • Photos for the online application follow a different spec (JPEG, RGB, 20 KB–7 MB, width and height 480–6000 pixels). App version 1.0 makes the printed L-size sheet.

How to print at a konbini (L size, 3 chains)

L size is 89 × 127 mm. The app saves one L-size image (1051 × 1500 px) with several copies of the photo, so you only print it as a normal photo print in L size and cut it out.

セブン-イレブン / 7-Eleven

  1. Send the saved L-size image from your phone to the copier. There are three apps: 'かんたんnetprint' (no registration; valid until the day after you reserve), 'netprint' (registration; valid 30 days after you reserve) and 'セブン-イレブン マルチコピー'. At the machine you can also scan a QR code to send the image over Wi-Fi.
  2. At the multi-copy machine choose 'Photo print' (写真プリント) and call up the image you sent (reservation number or QR code).
  3. Choose paper size 'L' (L判, 89 × 127 mm). Do not choose '証明写真プリント' (ID photo print): it is a separate, more expensive menu.
  4. The price is ¥40 per print. Cut along the cutting lines with scissors.

ローソン / Lawson

  1. Send the saved L-size image to the machine with the 'PrintSmash' app (choose Photo).
  2. At the multi-copy machine choose 'Photo print' (写真プリント) and call up the image you sent (reservation number or QR code).
  3. Choose paper size 'L' (L判, 89 × 127 mm). Do not choose '証明写真プリント' (ID photo print): it is a separate, more expensive menu.
  4. The price is ¥30 per print. Cut along the cutting lines with scissors.

ファミリーマート / FamilyMart

  1. Send the saved L-size image to the machine with the 'ファミマネットワークプリント' app (free) or 'PrintSmash'.
  2. At the multi-copy machine choose 'Photo print' (写真プリント) and call up the image you sent (reservation number or QR code).
  3. Choose paper size 'L' (L判, 89 × 127 mm). Do not choose '証明写真プリント' (ID photo print): it is a separate, more expensive menu.
  4. The price is ¥30 per print. Cut along the cutting lines with scissors.
Warning: '証明写真プリント' (ID photo print) is a separate menu and costs ¥200–250 for an L print. Normal photo print in L size is ¥30–40 per print. 7-Eleven states that 'ID photo print is not suitable for passport applications'.

Make it in the app

Taking the photo and previewing the watermarked result are free. Only saving the L-size sheet costs money: a one-time ¥500 for all uses (no subscription, no ads, no login). Photos are processed on your phone only and are never uploaded.
