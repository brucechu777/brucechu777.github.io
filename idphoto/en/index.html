ID photo sizes in Japan: official requirements for 5 uses

These pages collect the official ID photo requirements for procedures in Japan, one page per use. Each page starts with the size, head size, background and source. The app '証明写真 - 在留カード・ビザ・免許' makes the photo to these specifications and saves a sheet you can print at a convenience store in L size.

The 5 uses

  • Residence card (renewal, change of status, permanent residence) — 30 × 40 mm
  • Driver's license (foreign license conversion, renewal) — 24 × 30 mm
  • My Number Card / Japanese passport — 35 × 45 mm
  • Chinese passport / visa — 33 × 48 mm
  • Résumé (rirekisho) — 30 × 40 mm

How to print at a konbini (L size, 3 chains)

  • セブン-イレブン / 7-Eleven: L ¥40
  • ローソン / Lawson: L ¥30
  • ファミリーマート / FamilyMart: L ¥30

Make it in the app

Taking the photo and previewing the watermarked result are free. Only saving the L-size sheet costs money: a one-time ¥500 for all uses (no subscription, no ads, no login). Photos are processed on your phone only and are never uploaded.
