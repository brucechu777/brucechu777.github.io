Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

  • In the app '証明写真 - 在留カード・ビザ・免許', photos are processed only on your device. Photos are never sent or uploaded to a server.
  • There is no account and no login, and there are no ads. The developer does not receive any of your personal data.
  • Face detection and person cut-out use Google's ML Kit, which is built into the app. Under Google's terms, ML Kit may send Google diagnostic and usage information such as device and app information (model, OS version, app version), a per-installation identifier and performance metrics. Google states it uses this to diagnose and improve ML Kit and does not transfer it to third parties. The photos themselves are not sent.
  • The purchase (one-time ¥500) and purchase history are handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive card or other payment details.
  • The camera permission is used only when you take a photo. Only images you choose to save are written to your photo library.
