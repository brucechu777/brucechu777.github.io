What the app does

It makes ID photos for 5 uses to the official specifications: residence card, driver's license (gaimen kirikae), My Number card / Japanese passport, Chinese passport / visa, and résumé. It changes the background to white, crops to size, checks the head size and more, and saves the photos on one L-size (89 × 127 mm) sheet that you can print as a photo print at a convenience store.

Restoring your purchase

Unlocking saving is a one-time ¥500 purchase, not a subscription. After changing phones or reinstalling, sign in to Google Play with the same Google account you used to buy, open the app and tap 'Restore purchase' on the result screen. The same account is never charged twice.On iPhone, sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy, then tap 'Restore purchase'.
