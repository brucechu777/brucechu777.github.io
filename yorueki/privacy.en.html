Night Station Escape - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Summary

Escape Game: Night Station (com.brucechu.yorueki) is an Android app. It has no accounts or login, and the developer runs no server. It uses no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

What stays on your device

Game progress, settings and whether you bought Remove Ads are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes them.

Notifications

The "new station" reminder is a local notification created on your device. It appears only if you allow it, and the app uses no server push notifications. You can change the permission in your device settings at any time.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), the only third-party service in the app. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use data such as the advertising ID (Google advertising ID), IP address, device information and ad interactions.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

On Android you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, under Settings > Google (or Privacy) > Ads. Before ads first appear you may see a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform.

Remove Ads

If you buy Remove Ads (one-time, ¥300 in Japan), the ads SDK is not initialized and the app makes no ad-related requests. The purchase is handled by Google Play; the developer never receives your payment details.
