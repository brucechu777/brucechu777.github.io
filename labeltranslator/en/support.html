What the app does

Japan Food Label Translator reads the ingredient list (原材料名) on a Japanese food package from a photo, translates every ingredient into English, Bahasa Indonesia or Bahasa Melayu, and highlights the words you chose to avoid: pork and alcohol by default, and optionally all meat, seafood, dairy, egg and any of Japan's 29 food allergens. Red = printed on the label; amber = source not stated; grey = debated (only if you turn it on). It is not a halal certification and only shows words printed on the label.

If a word is missing

Take the photo closer, with the 原材料名 box filling the frame, even light and no glare. Small or curved print can be misread. If nothing is found, the app reminds you that pork, beef, chicken and gelatin are optional to declare in Japan.

Restoring your purchase

Unlimited scans are a one-time purchase (¥600 in Japan), not a subscription. After changing phones or reinstalling, sign in to Google Play with the same Google account you used to buy, open the app and tap “Restore purchase” in Settings or on the unlock page. The same account is never charged twice.
