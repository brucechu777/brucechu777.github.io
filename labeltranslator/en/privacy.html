Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

  • In Japan Food Label Translator, photos are read on your phone. Photos are never uploaded or sent to the developer or anyone else. Scan history (a small picture, the text that was read and the words found) is stored only on your phone, and you can delete it in History.
  • There is no account and no login, and there are no ads or analytics from the developer. The developer does not receive any of your personal data.
  • On Android, text recognition uses Google's ML Kit, which is built into the app. Under Google's terms, ML Kit sends Google diagnostic and usage information: device information (such as manufacturer, model and OS version), app information (package name and version), a per-installation identifier, performance metrics, API configuration and error codes. Google states it encrypts this in transit and does not transfer it to third parties. The photos and the text read from them are not sent.
  • The one-time purchase (¥600 in Japan) and purchase history are handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive card or other payment details. The app also connects to Google Play to show the system rating prompt.
  • The camera permission is used only while you take a photo of a label. Choosing a photo uses the system photo picker, so the app only sees the photo you pick.
  • The iPhone version (if available) uses Apple's Vision, built into iOS, instead of ML Kit, and sends no information to the developer, Google or any other third party; purchases there are handled by the App Store.
