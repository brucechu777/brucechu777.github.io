How to read a Japanese ingredient label (原材料名)

How the ingredient list (原材料名) on Japanese food is written, where pork, alcohol and gelatin can appear, and why “not on the label” does not mean “not used”. With official sources.

Where to look

Turn the package over and find the box headed 原材料名 (genzairyō-mei, “ingredient names”). Ingredients are listed by weight, the largest first, using their common names. Osaka Prefecture (大阪府), 加工食品の原材料名

Brackets hide more ingredients

A compound ingredient is followed by its own ingredients in brackets, for example マーガリン(パーム油、豚脂、食塩…) — margarine made with 豚脂 (pork fat). Always read inside the brackets. Osaka Prefecture (大阪府), 加工食品の原材料名

Additives come after “/” or in their own line

Additives are either listed in a separate 添加物 line, or after a slash ／ at the end of the ingredients, for example …食塩／ソルビトール、酒精、乳化剤、膨張剤、香料. 酒精 is ethanol; 乳化剤 (emulsifier) and 香料 (flavouring) do not say what they are made from. Consumer Affairs Agency (消費者庁), 食物アレルギー表示ハンドブック 令和8年4月

The allergen line: (一部に…を含む)

Allergens are shown either after each ingredient, like (豚肉を含む), or all together at the end: (一部に小麦・卵・乳成分・大豆・豚肉を含む) — “contains in part wheat, egg, milk, soy, pork”. Consumer Affairs Agency (消費者庁), 食物アレルギー表示ハンドブック 令和8年4月

Required (9): えび shrimp, カシューナッツ cashew (required since 2026-04-01; old labels may be used until 2028-03-31), かに crab, くるみ walnut, 小麦 wheat, そば buckwheat, 卵 egg, 乳 milk, 落花生 peanut. Consumer Affairs Agency (消費者庁), 事務連絡 令和8年4月1日 (カシューナッツ) · Osaka Prefecture (大阪府), 食品表示 (カシューナッツの経過措置)

Recommended (20): アーモンド, あわび, いか, いくら, オレンジ, キウイフルーツ, 牛肉 beef, ごま, さけ, さば, 大豆, 鶏肉 chicken, バナナ, ピスタチオ (added 2026-04-01), 豚肉 pork, マカダミアナッツ, もも, やまいも, りんご, ゼラチン gelatin. Consumer Affairs Agency (消費者庁), 食物アレルギー表示ハンドブック 令和8年4月

“Not on the label” does not mean “not used”

Pork, beef, chicken and gelatin are only recommended allergen declarations, so a label without (豚肉を含む) does not prove that there is no pork. Consumer Affairs Agency (消費者庁), 食物アレルギー表示ハンドブック 令和8年4月

For allergen labelling, “pork” covers the meat itself and animal fat such as ラード (lard). Gelatin “is often made mainly from cattle or pigs, but some is made from fish”, so the word ゼラチン alone does not tell you the animal. Consumer Affairs Agency (消費者庁), 食物アレルギー表示ハンドブック 令和8年4月

In allergen labelling, general words such as 肉類 (meats) or 動物性〇〇 (animal …) may not be used instead of naming beef, pork or chicken. In the ingredient list itself, words such as 肉エキス (meat extract) or 動物性油脂 (animal fat) still do not tell you which animal. Consumer Affairs Agency (消費者庁), 食物アレルギー表示ハンドブック 令和8年4月
