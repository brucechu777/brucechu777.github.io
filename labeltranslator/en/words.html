Japanese words for pork, alcohol and gelatin on food labels

The app looks for these words in the ingredient list and shows them in the original Japanese text. The explanations are one factual sentence each and say what the word is, not whether you may eat it. Allergen words (egg, milk, wheat, shrimp…) are also in the app but not repeated here.
