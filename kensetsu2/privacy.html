Privacy Policy – Tokutei 2 Construction 建設2号 学科

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Summary

A practice app for the written part (学科) of the Specified Skilled Worker (ii) construction exam. It has no account system, no server of its own, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

What stays on your device

Your answers, mock exams, daily questions and settings (language, furigana, reminder time) are saved only on your device; we never receive them. If you reinstall with the same Google account, Android's own backup may restore them.

Reminder notifications

If you turn on the reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules reminders on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether you own the unlock.

Ratings

The app may show the Google Play rating dialog. Ratings are handled by Google.

Error reports and contact

Tapping report or contact opens your email app with a prepared draft. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.
