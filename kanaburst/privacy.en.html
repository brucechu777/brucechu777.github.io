Kana Burst: Learn Hiragana
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Summary

Kana Burst: Learn Hiragana is a small offline-first game for learning hiragana. It has no account system, no server of its own, no analytics and no crash reporting.

What stays on your device

Your learning progress and settings (such as sound and language) are saved only on your device. We never receive them. They stay on your device until you clear the app's data or use Reset progress in Settings. If you reinstall the app with the same Google account, Android's own backup may restore them. All sounds are pre-recorded and bundled inside the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may collect and use data such as your advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads, as described in Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where required, a consent form from Google User Messaging Platform is shown so you can choose how your data is used; you can change that choice later from the app settings, and you can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases

This version has no in-app purchases.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
