Privacy Policy – Forklift & Tamakake Written Test Practice (Japan)

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Summary

A practice app for the written part (学科) of the forklift operator skill training and the Tamakake (crane slinging) skill training in Japan. It has no account system, no server of its own, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. We do not collect any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your answers, mock exam results and settings (language, furigana, translation display) are saved only on your device; we never receive them.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone). We never receive your payment details; the app only asks the store whether you own the unlock.

Ratings

The app may show the Google Play or App Store rating dialog. Ratings are handled by Google / Apple.

Error reports and contact

Tapping report or contact opens your email app with a prepared draft. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.
